Biography

I am a statistical scientist and Postdoctoral Fellow at the Ottawa Hospital Research Institute. My work brings together statistical methodology, causal inference, and data science.

I earned my PhD in Statistics at the University of Manitoba, where I developed kernel-based methods for goodness-of-fit testing and studied Bayesian inference. My postdoctoral work includes survival analysis, health economic modelling, and analysis of clinical and administrative health data.

I also have experience in banking analytics, including forecasting, customer analysis, and business intelligence.
